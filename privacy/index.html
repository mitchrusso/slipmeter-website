Privacy policy

Your work records remain yours.

SlipMeter is local-first. Cloud synchronization is optional, transparent, and limited to the information needed to provide SlipMeter Network.

Effective November 21, 2026

Who operates SlipMeter

SlipMeter is operated by Mindful Guidance MR, LLC. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to support@clickcoach.io.

Information stored on your device

SlipMeter can be used without connecting to a cloud account. Clients, activities, timer state, comments, and saved slips are stored locally on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad. Removing the app removes its local application data unless it was separately exported, backed up by the device, or synchronized to SlipMeter Network.

Information collected when you use SlipMeter Network

If you choose to connect SlipMeter Network, we process the account and work-record information needed to provide that service:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your organization, membership, and registered-device information.
  • Completed slips you choose to synchronize, including client, activity, comments, dates, duration, classifications, outcomes, and review status.
  • Security and operational records such as sign-in attempts, synchronization status, and audit events.

We use this information only for account access, device control, synchronization, reporting, support, fraud prevention, and service security.

Where Network data is stored

SlipMeter Network is hosted on Cloudflare. Network traffic is encrypted in transit with HTTPS, and hosted data is protected by the platform's encryption at rest and access controls. Device credentials are stored in the Apple Keychain on iPhone and iPad and in protected system storage on Mac.

No advertising or tracking

SlipMeter does not sell personal information, use third-party advertising SDKs, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or use your work records for behavioral advertising.

Sharing and service providers

We disclose information only to service providers that help operate SlipMeter, such as hosting, transactional email, error diagnosis, and customer support, or when required by law. These providers may use the information only to perform services for us.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. Network records remain available while the associated service is active. After cancellation, Network data is retained for up to 30 days to allow recovery and is then deleted or de-identified, except where a longer period is required for security, dispute resolution, or law.

To request deletion of a SlipMeter Network account and its associated data, email support@clickcoach.io from the account's email address. We may verify the request before completing it.

Your choices

  • Use SlipMeter locally without connecting Network.
  • Disconnect a device and stop future synchronization.
  • Export or delete local slips.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion of Network information.

Children

SlipMeter is a professional productivity product and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as SlipMeter changes. Material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.
