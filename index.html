Ask a different question
Does SlipMeter work on its own?

Yes. SlipMeter is a standalone Mac app. Create local clients and activities, save slips, run reports, and export your data without another platform.

What happens if I close the app while a timer is running?

SlipMeter warns you before closing. The elapsed time is stopped and the current slip is saved so work is not silently lost.

Can I change clients while a timer is active?

Yes. SlipMeter asks for confirmation, saves the current slip, and resets the timer for the new client.

Can I edit time or review pending slips?

Yes. You can edit elapsed time, identify running and pending slips, and complete or save records from the app.

Can I export my records?

Yes. Export detailed records, including comments and classifications, for reporting, analysis, invoicing preparation, or consolidation.

Is the app safe to install?

Yes. The current macOS installer is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
